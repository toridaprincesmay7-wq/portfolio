About Me

A young student with big dreams, multiple passions, and a heart for learning and leadership.

I am Jasmine Antonio , a Grade 10 student who believes that education is one of the strongest foundations for achieving dreams.

I am proud to be a consistent honor student . Maintaining academic excellence while participating in extracurricular activities has taught me the value of time management, discipline, perseverance, and responsibility.

MY SECOND TITLE • 2025

Ms. Binibini 2025

One of the most memorable milestones in my journey is earning my second pageant title as Ms. Binibini 2025 .


For me, pageantry is more than beauty and wearing a crown. It is about confidence, character, communication, discipline, responsibility, and purpose.
